# True Motu: Philosophy

What this project is, and the yardstick every feature is checked against
(`/vision-check`). Edit this when the picture changes; don't work around it.

**What it is:** a community-built dictionary of True Motu (Motu korikori, ISO 639-3 `meu`),
the endangered Austronesian language of the Port Moresby region. It's built so fluent
speakers can pass the language on before it's lost, and so other endangered languages can
reuse the platform.

## Principles

1. **Speakers are the authority.** Fluent native and heritage speakers decide what a word
   means. The 1973 Bible text and AI suggestions are prompts to get people started, never
   the answer.
2. **True Motu, not Hiri Motu.** Protect the language's integrity. Pidgin, Tok Pisin and
   English must not drift in as Motu.
3. **Capture the living language.** Record how Motu is actually spoken today, including
   regional and generational variants, not just what the Bible corpus contains. A
   disagreement can be a real variant, not an error.
4. **Built for elders on phones.** One thing at a time, plain words, few taps, and it has
   to work on a slow PNG mobile connection.
5. **Protect the people who contribute.** Consent decides how contributions are used.
   Children are safeguarded. Nothing private leaks.
6. **The community keeps it clean, not an auditor.** Abuse, bad language and deliberately
   wrong entries are caught and contained automatically by the community's own signals
   (consensus, trust, flags). Trevor is told about exceptions; he doesn't review everything.
7. **Open, cheap, reusable.** It runs for about $0/month, the data can be exported, and
   nothing is so specific to Motu that another language couldn't reuse it.

## Fit test

Answer each in one line for any new feature:

1. Does it put fluent speakers' knowledge first, or substitute text or AI for it?
2. Could it let non-Motu (Hiri Motu, Tok Pisin, English) in as Motu?
3. Does it leave room for real variants, or punish disagreement as error?
4. Does it work for an elder on a phone with a slow connection?
5. Does it respect consent and protect minors?
6. Can it be abused, and does the community contain that without Trevor auditing?
7. Does it keep costs near zero and the data open?
